About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK) and Humboldt University of Berlin, supported by the CSC-DAAD Postdoctoral Scholarship and hosted by Prof. Jürgen Kurths.

My research focuses on higher-order interactions, synchronization, nonlinear and stochastic dynamics, extreme events, bifurcation, chaos, and Lévy-noise-driven systems.
